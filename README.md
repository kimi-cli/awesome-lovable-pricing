# Awesome Lovable pricing

*Unofficial community list for Lovable pricing. Not affiliated with Lovable. All trademarks belong to their owners.*

A curated list of pages worth reading before you commit to a plan. Lovable pricing is credit-based, with a free daily grant, a monthly Cloud grant and a paid balance that expires on its own schedule, and the details are spread across the pricing page, the docs and a few outside write-ups. This list groups them so you can find the answer to a billing question in one hop.

## Official resources

- [Pricing](https://lovable.dev/pricing) - the plans page and the FAQ that defines what a credit is, the free plan grants and the expiry rules.
- [Credits and usage](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage) - the docs page on where credits come from (usage-specific grants versus general credits) and what usage costs.
- [Subscription plans](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/subscription-plans) - the docs entry for each plan tier.
- [Enterprise](https://lovable.dev/enterprise-landing) - the enterprise offering for teams that need more than the self-serve tiers.
- [Lovable for Enterprise (docs)](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/lovable-for-enterprise) - the documentation side of the enterprise plan.
- [Support policy](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/support-policy) - what support each plan gets.
- [Support](https://lovable.dev/support) - where to open a billing ticket.
